      How do I export?
      • Strava: open an activity → ⋯ menu → Export GPX.
      • Apple Health: Profile → Export All Health Data. Unzip it; your routes are in the workout-routes folder.
      • Google Fit: takeout.google.com → Fit → download. Your workouts are .tcx files in Activities.

            Privacy
            
          
              
            
            • Everything stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server.
              • 
            
            • Your PIN is never saved, only a slow, salted hash of it.
              • 
            
            • 5 wrong PINs lock the account for 1 minute. You are logged out after 15 minutes of no use.
              • 
            
            • Only the text you type is sent to Gemini, and only if you add a key in Settings.
