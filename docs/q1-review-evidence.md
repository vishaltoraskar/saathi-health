# Q1 evidence: 72 low-rated app reviews

All reviews are real 1- and 2-star reviews from Google Play, read on 1 November 2026 using the store's star-rating filter (sorted by "Most relevant"). Healthify and MyFitnessPal were read on the India store, Noom on the US store. Each review is **summarised in my own words** (not quoted) and coded for the problems it describes; one review can have several codes. Reviewer names are left out for privacy.

**Codes:** M = Money (paywall, upsell, ads/pop-ups, billing or cancellation traps) · A = Accuracy (wrong or missing food data, wrong AI estimates) · E = Effort (logging takes too long, extra clicks, removed shortcuts) · B = Bugs (crashes, lost data, sync or login failures) · S = Service (support or coaches absent or generic) · P = Personal fit (plan or targets do not adapt to the person) · U = Update broke it (a change removed or broke something the user relied on) · L = Long-time user (said they used it for 6+ months)

**Sources:** [Healthify](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.healthifyme.basic) · [MyFitnessPal](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.myfitnesspal.android) · [Noom](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wsl.noom)
